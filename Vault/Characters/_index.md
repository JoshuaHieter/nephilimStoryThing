### The 1500s crew

- [[The Chaplain-Knight]] — **Don Bartolomeo della Croce** — leader; Italian survivor of Rhodes, zealot, most loyal to the Pope
- [[The Lutheran]] — **Konrad Schreiber** — belligerent mid-20s super-genius; self-taught Hebraist & Luther disciple; the one who can crack the diary
- [[The Captain]] — **Captain Edmund Drake** — renowned English sea-captain, early 40s; nominally Catholic, brought because his crew is the best
- [[The Pirate]] — **Mateus Corvo** — Portuguese street-urchin turned reluctant voodoo-pirate; the only one who's crossed and lived
