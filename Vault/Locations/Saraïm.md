# Saraïm

> The dominant power of [[The Ocean World|Rephaim]]: a **theocratic, ethno-fascist** empire — pure-[[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]] nobility ruling as both throne and church, hard-oppressing a patchwork of conquered Kilayim races, and certain by blood that they are destined to rule the stars.

## At a glance

- **Type:** large **aristocratic empire / theocracy** — the continent's dominant power; the ruling pureblood caste *is* the church (church = state)
- **Territory:** the **centre** of Rephaim, spanning a good deal more land than [[Gibborim]] or [[Toldoth]]
- **Name:** from *sar*, "noble / prince" → "the Nobles"
- **Rulers:** a pure **Tahorim** aristocracy
- **Subjects:** **many different Kilayim** (hybrid) races, **oppressed hard**

## Power & control

- A rigid aristocracy of pure-blood **Tahorim** lords sitting atop **many subject hybrid races.**
- Oppression is **harsh and systemic** — the Kilayim are an exploited underclass, held by the fear-and-domination logic of [[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Nephilim society]].
- *(Real-world rhyme: **theocratic ethno-fascism** — a master-race ideology fused to the state, a colonial empire of many crushed peoples under one "pure" caste.)*

## Law & the Kilayim

### Legal status: serfdom with local autonomy
- Conquered Kilayim are **serfs**.
- Autonomy is granted **not to a race as a whole**, but to the **small tribe that was conquered**: it may **police itself a little**, the way **Rome handled its subject peoples**.
- *(This is why buying free [[Gibborim]] is frowned upon: conquered subjects hold a defined place under the law, while the unconquered have none.)*

### Keeping order
- **No divide-and-rule.** Order is mostly **self-policing** within each conquered tribe.
- Each tribe has a **Kilayim governor** of its own kind. It usually falls to **him** to put down unrest among his people, who see him as a **traitor**.
- **Revolts are rare.** When they happen, **warlike Kilayim** are brought in to crush them.

### The Kilayim in the faith
- **Service is worship.** Kilayim venerate the Tahorim as **living relics** of the fallen angels, and approach the stars **through their masters**.
- **Sanctified roles.** **Each Kilayim race belongs to one constellation**, whose angel sanctifies its caste role: *your servitude is your angel's will.* (Roster: see [[Kilayim Races]].)

### Mixing: one law for men, another for women
- **Tahor men with Kilayim women: not a crime.** Some Kilayim women are **prostituted** or kept as **"comfort maids."**
  - **Their sons are neutered** and used as **eunuch liaisons** to their mother's race.
  - **Daughters are aborted.** In secret, their organs are a **delicacy among the elite.**
- **Tahor women with Kilayim men** are condemned as **"whoring after animalistic pleasures."** The punishment is **public execution by rape at the hands of a Kilayim executioner**, both as punishment and as a warning.

## The subject races (the Kilayim underclass)

The empire runs on a **caste-stack of eight** [[Kilayim Races|Kilayim]] races, **one per constellation**:

- **Kelevim** (hound-folk; Azazel): soldiers; crush revolts.
- **Atalefim** (bat-folk; Kokabiel): night-watch, lookouts, navigators' aides.
- **Shiqtzim**, "the Abominations" (eel-folk, who call themselves [[Ruakei]]; Baraqiel): dock, dive and sea labour.
- **Orevim** (raven-folk; Penemue): scribes and messengers.
- **Rachamim** (vulture-folk; Sariel): undertakers and public executioners, "the Mercies."
- **Tinshamim** (chameleon-folk; Armaros): spies and informers.
- **Arnavim** (hare-folk; Gadreel): comfort maids, concubines, wet-nurses, midwives.
- **Chazirim** (boar-folk; Araqiel): miners, farmers, haulers.

**Accepting the sanctified role** is how a Kilayim leaves the **backwater governorship** for the **big city or a great estate**, and gets **paid**.

Full roster and stock in [[Kilayim Races]]. Many also live as **free** communities in the [[The Ocean World|Iyim]] — the same race, crushed here and sovereign there.

**On the map:** Saraïm holds the central island plus four nearby large islands, **one nationality per island**. The **Crown Isle** is the central island, with [[Zebul]]. The [[The Saraïm Faith|pantheon]] is shared by all of them and **not tied to any nationality**.

## Religion = state: theocratic ethno-fascism

*(Each Nephilim power braids faith, culture, and its Earth-story into one thing. The Saraïm's is the harshest — a **true religion** fused to a master-race ideology, where the **church is the state.**)*

The Saraïm are **genuinely religious**: a faith of **waiting**, with their bound lord shining over them every night. Day-to-day practice, the pantheon and the sky are set out in [[The Saraïm Faith]]. Their creed:

- **We are descended from the Morning Star.** The [[The Morning Star|Morning Star]] — Yahweh's great rebel — is their **progenitor:** they are the heirs of **the Morning Star's sons (the fallen angels) and Earth's daughters (human women).** That blood is their birthright and their pride.
- **The Morning Star lives, but is bound.** He is the **pole star** of their sky, the fixed point all the heavens turn around. Its light proves he is **not dead**. Instead, he is **trapped by Yahweh on Earth**, which they believe is still a **drowned world of all sea and no land** (Noah's Flood never receded). He is **out of reach**, so what remains is the inheritance: the **stars he won them** and the **mandate to conquer them.**
- **We are the purebloods; we will rule the stars.** Bloodline purity is destiny. The pure **[[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]]** are the rightful masters of creation, carrying their bound progenitor's conquest onward.
- **You are lowly Kilayim — bow down.** The other face is brutal contempt for the [[Kilayim Races|hybrid underclass]]: lesser blood, born to serve and grovel.

So it's **sincere worship welded to ethno-fascism with a cosmic mandate**, a theocracy where the **ruling pureblood caste *is* the church**. At bottom: *our lord, our blood, our stars, your knees.*

**Minority views.** Binding is the **main** theory, not the only one. A heretical school holds that **his light outlives him**: he is dead, and the star is only his light still arriving. Toldoth thinks he is simply **dead** ([[Toldoth]]).

> **Truth vs. belief (the explosive secret):** they are **right that he lives and is caged on Earth**, but **wrong about Earth.** Earth is **not a drowned waste**. It is **habitable**, full of land and people, and **the Morning Star is seriously reachable** by following his star ([[The Morning Star]], [[The Crossing]]). The crew are living proof.

**The dread scenario:** if the Saraïm ever learn that **Earth is habitable and their bound lord can actually be reached**, the inheritance becomes a **summons.** Manifest-destiny conquest turns into a **holy crusade to reach him** ([[The Morning Star|→ invasion]]). Stopping that knowledge is the crew's deeper job.

## The throne (top of the chain)

The empire's supreme rule belongs to the **purest, most ancient line of all:** whoever can trace their ancestry to the **pre-flood King of Canaan** — the legendary antediluvian giant-king of the old giant-land, the most prestigious Nephilim royal blood there is. His line **fled the Flood** ([[Nephilim & the Flood]]) and founded the Saraïm dynasty; to sit the throne, you must **prove descent from him.** It is the **ultimate purity claim** — the apex of the whole ideology.

This ruler is the **Melek** — in full, ***Melek ha-Kokhavim*, King of the Stars** (the cosmic-conquest mandate baked right into the title). He rules from the capital **[[Zebul]]**, and is the **top of the chain the secret races toward** ([[Book Plotline]]): the most ideologically-invested figure on Rephaim — the one who, learning the Morning Star lives, could turn the empire's whole might into a **crusade to reach him.**

**But the throne is a gilded cage.** Being the *purest* blood means being the *most inbred* ([[Nephilim Biology & Caste|the Habsburg curse]] at its peak): the emperor is a **degenerate, capricious god-king** — absolute in theory, but disengaged, given to random decrees and general cruelty, uninterested in the grind of rule. The ones who **actually run the empire are [[The Saraïm Priesthood|the priests]]**, who venerate him while keeping him distracted enough to govern in his name.

In the [[Book Plotline|book]], the crew race to keep the secret from this throne — and arrive **a beat too late**, and must kill the **emperor himself.**
