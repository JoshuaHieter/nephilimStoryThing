# Zebul

> The capital of [[Saraïm]] — seat of the **Melek (King of the Stars)** and of [[The Saraïm Priesthood|the priests]] who really rule. Its name hides a Devil in plain sight.

## At a glance

- The **throne-city** of the Saraïm empire, on [[The Ocean World|Rephaim]].
- Seat of the **Melek** (*[[Saraïm#The throne (top of the chain)|Melek ha-Kokhavim]]*) and the **priesthood.**
- The **climax setting** of the [[Book Plotline]]: where the crew kill the *sar* and the Melek.

## The name

- Hebrew *zebul*, **"the exalted dwelling / lofty abode"** — the high seat of the lord.
- The deep-cut: it's the root of **Ba'al-*zebul* → Beelzebub**, "lord of the exalted dwelling." So the Satan-descended empire's capital literally names **the Devil's high seat** — a horror hidden in plain sight that **only a Hebrew scholar would catch.** A chilling little beat for [[The Lutheran|Konrad]].
