# The Ocean World

> *(Name TBD.)* The alien world the crew is marooned on — the one [[The Pirate]] glimpsed as a boy, the home of [[The Outcast Scholar]], and the **most civilized, advanced, and least hostile** of the [[The Known Worlds|three alien worlds]] — yet ringed by deadly seas.

## At a glance

- One of the **three alien worlds**; its dominant biome is **ocean.**
- **The most civilized, most technologically advanced, and least hostile** of the three.
- Where [[The Pirate]] crossed to as a boy, where [[The Outcast Scholar]] came from, and where [[The Pope's Mission]] ends up stranded.

## Geography

- **Very oceanic.** The main continent — **Rephaim** — is almost an **archipelago**, but the islands are **decently large.** Think the **British Isles, but more** (bigger, and more of them). *(Rephaim: the biblical giant-dead/shades — a drowned-isle land of giants.)*
- The **islands themselves aren't terribly hostile** to live on. The danger is **getting between them.**

## The sea monsters

- The seas teem with **sea monsters** — broadly **like Earth's, but more aggressive and living closer to the surface.**
- Per [[Setting Principles]], they're **biologically believable**, not magical — aggressive megafauna, not krakens of myth.
- They make **sea travel dangerous** — the chief obstacle to moving around the archipelago, and the horror that **strands every crew that crosses** ([[The Pope's Mission]]).

### The apex: giant sea snakes
- The **apex predator** of the seas is a **giant sea snake**, **really big**.
- It kills **mainly by crushing**: a constrictor.
- **Biology:** a real snake family, alien genus. Real giant marine snakes existed (the extinct *Palaeophis*, roughly 9 m or more), and water carries weight, so a far larger one passes the biology-class test. Being air-breathers, they **must keep surfacing**, which is part of why Rephaim's monsters hunt **close to the surface**.

### The rest of the roster
Around the snakes, the seas also hold, each a real family with an alien genus:
- **Surface squid** (giant-squid family): rise at night to drag men off decks.
- **Ship-rammers** (sperm-whale family): territorial bulls that smash hulls.
- **Pod-hunters** (dolphin / orca family): clever packs that have learned boats mean food.
- **Sea-crocodiles** (crocodile family): the shallows and estuaries.
- **Sting-mats** (man-o'-war family): drifting stinging colonies that foul oars and can close a sea lane for a season.

*Suggestions (not yet canon):* the snakes called **Livyatan** or **Tanninim** by the Saraïm (Isa. 27:1; Gen. 1:21); the big deep-water hunters avoid the shallows, leaving them to crocodiles and sting-mats, which is why the eelfolk can work there.

## Technology

- The civilization is **slightly more advanced than 1500s Earth** — with one glaring exception: **no gunpowder, and no guns.**
- Instead: **better contraptions**, the **beginnings of industrial-revolution technology** — mechanically clever, gears-and-machines, but **nothing that goes bang.**
- *(The crew's **gunpowder** is their one edge — but a **finite, salvaged** one: the armed away-team kept their firearms and **sealed powder horns/flasks** (which stayed dry) when they fled the dying ship in the small boat. It **can't be replaced** here, so it's a **dwindling** edge — every shot counts — not an arsenal.)*

## Who lives here

- A **Nephilim civilization** ([[Nephilim Biology & Caste]]) — pure Nephilim as the revered upper caste, hybrid "common folk" beneath.
- The **Nephilim continent — Rephaim — is the most habitable spot on the whole world** — and it's exactly where the crossing-star deposits you. The crew arrives **right in Nephilim territory.** ("Behind enemy lines.")

## The powers of Rephaim

The habitable archipelago is divided among **three powers** and a scatter of independent isles:

- **[[Saraïm]]** — a vast **aristocratic empire**; pure-Tahorim nobility ruling and **hard-oppressing many different Kilayim subject races.** The dominant power.
- **[[Toldoth]]** — a realm of Nephilim and a **single** hybrid race, ranged across a **full gradient from pure to barely-Tahorim** — but they prize a specific **balance** of blood, not purity. Uneasy neighbours with [[Saraïm]].
- **[[Gibborim]]** — **no pure Tahorim at all** and **less advanced**, but its hybrid race is **freakishly strong.** A land of brute power.
- **The Iyim** — a scatter of **weaker, independent island city-states**, **each populated wholly by a single [[Saraïm]]-underclass race** ([[Kilayim Races]]) — free, but weak. *(Individual isles TBD.)*

Caste terms throughout: **[[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]]** (pure) and **Kilayim** (hybrid).
