# Toldoth

> A realm of one blended people — Nephilim and the crystal-antlered **Ayalim** — who are **not Tahor supremacists.** Instead they breed for a **specific ratio of blood**: the balance at which the Ayalim's strange electric sense is at its strongest.

## At a glance

- **Type:** a country of **one blended bloodline** — Nephilim + the **Ayalim** (stag-folk; [[Kilayim Races]])
- **Name:** from *toledot*, "generations / lineages"
- **Defining trait:** a **continuous gradient** from pure Tahorim down to heavily-hybrid Kilayim — and a culture that prizes **the balance**, not the top of the gradient
- **Territory:** the **third-biggest island** of Rephaim, and not much more
- **Relations:** **uneasy neighbours** with [[Saraïm]]

## The Ayalim & the wild ayal

Toldoth's one hybrid stock is the **Ayalim** (stag-folk; full detail in [[Kilayim Races]]): part **alien cervid — a great white stag with antlers of clear, living crystal (quartz).**

**The antlers are a sense organ.** Living quartz is *piezoelectric*, so the antlers act as antennae: they pick up **electrical fields** (heartbeat, flinching muscle, fear) and **pressure / vibration** (weather, footsteps). Like real electroreception, the sense is **short-range in air** — best up close, by touch, or on wet ground.

**Touch to disorient.** Laid near someone's head, an antler can make them **nauseous or uneasy** — and a **skilled** Ayal can make them **pass out.** (Grounded in real effects: induced currents cause vertigo, nausea, twitching.)

**The antlers do not shed.** They grow **mostly during puberty**, then only **very, very slowly** for the rest of life — so a **broken antler is effectively permanent.**

**Diet matters.** The crystal is built from **silica** the ayal takes in — silica-rich grasses, mineral springs — carried by special proteins and laid onto a collagen frame under living velvet. **What you eat shapes the antler you grow.**

**The wild ayal.** That same alien deer **roams the isles wild, in herds.** Not held *sacred* — but to **kill or eat one is deep taboo**, the way eating a pet dog would be in America: too close to kin, too much a companion. *(Cherished as a beloved **animal**, though — not believed a person, unlike the [[Tunoth]]'s sentient eel-cousins.)*

> **Crew hook:** a starving, marooned crew will see those herds as *food.* Butchering one among the Toldoth is a grave — maybe violent — offense. Survival vs. local taboo, head-on.

## The balance — what Toldoth breeds for

- The Toldoth are **not Tahor supremacists.** They breed toward a **specific ratio** of Tahor to ayal blood.
- **Too little animal:** an antlerless near-Tahor — no sense at all.
- **Too much:** the antlers flood the mind — sharp-sensed, but dim, out toward a near-full great stag.
- **The balance:** the sense is strong *and* the mind can still use it. That — not purity — is the Toldoth ideal.
- **The target is two-thirds Tahor.** It is hard to hit, because **breeding math doesn't work that way**: each generation halves, so pedigrees land on 1/2, 3/4, 5/8, 11/16… and only creep toward 2/3, never landing on it. Inheritance is also **a bit random**, so two children of the same parents can come out differently balanced. The ideal is always being chased and never guaranteed.
- Blood is still **read at a glance by the antlers** — but now the prized rack is the **right** one, not the biggest.

## Power & control

- Unlike [[Saraïm]]'s many subject races, here it's **one people**, ranked along a **spectrum of blood** — with the **balance**, not the pure end, at the top.
- *(Real-world rhyme: blood-quantum hierarchy within a single people — but aimed at a middle, not an extreme.)*

### The dual monarchy
- Toldoth is a **monarchy**: a **dual monarchy** of **two main kingdoms that formed together** into one realm. The two kingdoms **used to split the realm**, but over time the division has become a **formality with no real weight**.
- **Succession:** when the king dies, the **bloodlines present their best-balanced candidates**, and the next king is decided by a **contest using antlers**. It tests **sensing and processing**: how much a candidate can pick up through the antlers, and how well they make sense of it. It is the balance itself, put to trial.

## Religion, culture & the fear of Earth

- **A dead god.** The Toldoth are **not quite atheists**, but they **don't bother worshipping** the [[The Morning Star|Morning Star]] — to them the god is **dead.** Why pray to a corpse? *(The irony: he is very much alive, just caged on Earth.)*
- **A fervent, person-centred ideology.** No worship of the dead god, but a **fervent** creed centred on the **self**: **ascetic**, disciplined, devoted to personal growth.
- **The constellations as inner paths.** The same fallen-angel constellations the [[The Saraïm Faith|Saraïm]] worship are, to the Toldoth, something **akin to chakras**. Each one is an **area of personal growth** to be cultivated in oneself, not a god to pray to. The eight paths **map onto the Saraïm aspects, turned inward**:

| Constellation | Saraïm aspect (outward) | Toldoth path (inward), *draft* |
|---|---|---|
| Azazel | War & the Forge | discipline of the body; tempering oneself |
| Kokabiel | the Stars & Voyaging | the inner journey; finding one's bearing |
| Baraqiel | Storm & Sea | mastering passion and temper |
| Penemue | Writing & Memory | the mind; study and recollection |
| Sariel | the Moon, Tides & Death | acceptance of endings; facing one's death |
| Armaros | Secrets & Cunning | self-knowledge; seeing through one's own lies |
| Gadreel | Desire & the Taking of Brides | mastery of desire; choosing a match for the balance |
| Araqiel | Land & Dominion | self-rule; command of one's own life |

- **Ascetic practice.** **Fasting and strict diet regimens**: since diet shapes the antler, **self-denial and breeding are one project**. The **silica springs** serve as something like **monasteries**.
- **A weaker taboo on mixing** — mixing is the whole point, which is why Toldoth is a **fluid gradient** rather than a rigid caste.

## Why Toldoth breeds for balance

- **Why they rejected purity:** after [[History of Rephaim#3. The Wasting (the muddling event)|the Wasting]], a plague that struck the pure hardest, they looked at the inbred Tahorim (the Habsburg curse — [[Nephilim Biology & Caste]]) and concluded *purity is a sickness.*
- **Why the neighbours are uneasy:** to [[Saraïm]], breeding *down* from Tahor blood on purpose is a deliberate dilution of the holy blood — near-heresy.
- **Lineage ledgers:** true to the name ("generations / lineages"), they keep obsessive breeding records to steer each line toward the balance.
- **Silica as a resource:** because diet shapes the antler, silica-rich pastures and mineral springs are prized — and fought over.
