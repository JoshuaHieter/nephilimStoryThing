# Toldoth

> A realm of one blended people: Nephilim and a single hybrid race spread across a full gradient from pure [[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahor]] to barely-touched. Purity usually buys status — but not always.

## At a glance

- **Type:** a country of **one blended bloodline** — Nephilim + the **Ayalim** (stag-folk; [[Kilayim Races]])
- **Name:** from *toledot*, "generations / lineages"
- **Defining trait:** a **continuous gradient** from pure Tahorim down to heavily-hybrid Kilayim

## The Ayalim & the wild ayal

Toldoth's one hybrid stock is the **Ayalim** (stag-folk; full detail in [[Kilayim Races]]): part **alien cervid — a great white stag with antlers of clear, living crystal** — their blood **read by antler** (the bigger the glittering glass rack, the more Kilayim) — pure Tahor are tall, pale, antlerless geniuses; the more Kilayim carry ever-greater antlers, pelt and hoof, out to a near-full **great stag.** More animal buys sharper senses and speed at the cost of intellect — which is the **"not always"** in who rises.

**The wild ayal.** That same alien deer **roams the isles wild, in herds.** Not held *sacred* — but to **kill or eat one is deep taboo**, the way eating a pet dog would be in America: too close to kin, too much a companion. *(Cherished as a beloved **animal**, though — not believed a person, unlike the [[Tunoth]]'s sentient eel-cousins.)*

> **Crew hook:** a starving, marooned crew will see those herds as *food.* Butchering one among the Toldoth is a grave — maybe violent — offense. Survival vs. local taboo, head-on.

## Power & control

- Unlike [[Saraïm]]'s many subject races, here it's **one people**, ranked along a **spectrum of blood.**
- **The more pure, the higher the status — usually.** But *not always*: there's room for the exception — a hybrid who rises, a pure-blood who falls.
- *(Real-world rhyme: colorism / blood-quantum hierarchy within a single people.)*

## Religion, culture & the fear of Earth

*(Each power braids faith, culture, and its Earth-story differently. This is the Toldoth's — nearly the [[Saraïm]]'s inverse.)*

- **A dead god.** The Toldoth are **not quite atheists**, but they **don't bother worshipping** the [[The Morning Star|Morning Star]] — to them the god is **dead.** Why pray to a corpse? *(The irony: he is very much alive, just caged on Earth.)*
- **Superiority without scripture.** They hold the pure **[[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]]** superior not by divine right but **because they simply *are*** — plainly **smarter**, stronger of mind. A cold, empirical supremacy, no god required.
- **A weaker taboo on mixing.** Because their case is practical rather than holy, the **prohibition on interbreeding isn't strong** — which is exactly why Toldoth is a **fluid gradient** rather than a rigid caste.
- **Fear of Earth:** *(TBD — likely dismissive rather than fearful; if the gods are dead myths, Earth is probably just another dead myth to them.)*

This makes them a fascinating opposite to the Saraïm: same belief in pure-blood rule, but stripped of the Satanic faith — supremacy as **fact**, not **mandate.**

- What lets someone **rise against** their blood — wealth, deeds, cunning, beauty?
