# Gibborim

> The brute power of [[The Ocean World|Rephaim]]: no pure Tahorim at all, and less advanced — but its hybrid race is freakishly, inexplicably strong. Genesis's own word for the mighty.

## At a glance

- **Type:** a larger country with **no pure Nephilim** and **less technological advancement**
- **Name:** *gibborim*, "mighty men" — the Bible's word for the Nephilim's powerful offspring
- **Defining trait:** its hybrid race — the **Reëmim**, ox-centaurs ([[Kilayim Races]]) — is **extraordinarily physically powerful**
- **Society:** **very tribalistic** — clans and warbands, not a unified state
- **Minds:** **low intelligence** — brawn over brains, far below the ~120 IQ of pure [[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]]

## Power & control

- With **no Tahorim** overlords, power here runs on **raw physical might**, not bloodline-worship.
- Less advanced than [[Saraïm]] or [[Toldoth]], but its people are **dangerously strong** — a martial, brute-force culture.
- *(Real-world rhyme: power resting on force itself rather than inherited rank.)*

## Religion, culture & the fear of Earth

*(Each power braids faith, culture, and its Earth-story differently. This is the Gibborim's.)*

- **A pantheon that isn't there.** They worship a sprawl of **tribal gods who simply don't exist** — invented spirits and idols, none of them real. *(In this cosmology only the Judeo-Christian powers are real — [[The Morning Star|Satan]], the fallen angels, Yahweh. The Gibborim, lowest of mind, pray hardest to the emptiest heaven.)*
- **Tribalism over empire.** No purebloods, no throne — power is **clan, warband, and the strength of the strongest arm.** Monstrously powerful, but **fractious**, never unifying.
- **Low cunning, high muscle.** Their **dullness** keeps them from the schemes of [[Saraïm]] or [[Toldoth]] — but makes them terrifying in a straight fight.
- **Fear of Earth:** *(TBD — likely a crude tribal taboo or boogeyman-myth, not doctrine.)*

The grim irony: the Saraïm worship a god who is real; the Gibborim, the dimmest, worship gods who never were.
