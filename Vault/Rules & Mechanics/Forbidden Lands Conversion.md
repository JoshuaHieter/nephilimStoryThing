# Forbidden Lands Conversion

> A heavily-customized **Forbidden Lands** (Year Zero Engine) hack for this setting — the ruleset for the [[TTRPG Plotline]]. *(Homebrew for the table. If ever published, build on the Year Zero Engine third-party license, not Free League's text.)*

## Why Forbidden Lands fits
- **Survival sandbox** — stranded, fend-for-yourself, hex-by-hex. Matches "marooned behind enemy lines."
- **Low magic** — easy to strip to *no* PC magic (our [[Setting Principles]]).
- **Lethal, gritty combat** — fragile mortals vs. giants and monsters = *giants and grasshoppers.*
- **Resource dice** — perfect for our **finite, dwindling gunpowder** ([[The Ocean World]]).

## What we keep vs. change
- **Keep the engine:** Year Zero attributes (Strength, Agility, Wits, Empathy), skills, the **dice pool + pushing**, conditions, the **journey/quarter-day** survival system, strongholds.
- **Reskin/replace:** Kin, professions, talents, **magic → faith (or nothing)**, gear/weapons (firearms + alien tech), the bestiary, the map.

## Build modules *(to design, one at a time)*
- [ ] **Kin / ancestries** — who you can play (human-only? also Kilayim?).
- [ ] **Professions** — classes reskinned for 1520s humans (priest/crusader, scholar, captain, rogue/pirate, soldier…).
- [ ] **Talents** — abilities (cut magic talents; add faith/firearm/survival ones).
- [ ] **Faith vs. magic** — replace FL magic with a **non-supernatural faith/zeal** subsystem, or nothing.
- [ ] **Gear & weapons** — period arms, **firearms + the finite-powder rule**, alien gears-and-steam tech, no enemy guns.
- [ ] **Survival & travel** — the journey system reskinned for **marsh / archipelago / badlands** (and the sea-monster seas).
- [ ] **Combat & monsters** — YZE combat; firearms; how a **giant/Reëmim/sea-monster** outclasses a party.
- [ ] **Bestiary** — Kilayim, sea monsters, pure Nephilim, the Gibborim brutes.
- [ ] **The region / map** — **Rephaim** as the hexcrawl; adventure sites; the Gibborim opening.
- [ ] **Strongholds & downtime** — a hidden camp / captured site.
- [ ] **Campaign frame** — the stranded sandbox; PCs = new humans or the named crew?

## Foundational decisions (settle first)
1. **Who do you play?** Human-only (stranded crew / fresh humans), or **also playable Kilayim** (eelfolk, etc.)?
2. **Magic → faith, or nothing?** (Very low magic — no spellcasting. Faith could give grit/resolve bonuses, not fireballs.)
3. **Firearms** as a **finite supply die** that ticks down — confirm.
4. **How custom?** "Extensive" = rebuild Kin/Professions/Gear/Bestiary from scratch, keep the engine.
