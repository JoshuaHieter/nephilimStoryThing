# Rephaim map

The working map of **Rephaim**, the main continent of [[The Ocean World]]. Joshua approved this island layout on 2026-11-08.

![[Maps/Rephaim/rephaim-powers.png]]

![[Maps/Rephaim/rephaim-biomes.png]]

## The layout Joshua asked for

- A circle big enough to hold **Spain, France and the UK**: about **2,700 km across** (roughly Gibraltar to the top of Scotland).
- It's filled with islands the size of **Ireland and England-Scotland**, and the **closer to the centre, the bigger** they are. The big ones were then shrunk by about a third, so the largest is now a little smaller than Great Britain (~165,000 km²) and they run down to ~25,000 km².
- **Small islands scattered** around, mostly in the channels **between** the big ones, then enlarged (most are 20–80 km across).
- Land is about 28% of the circle. The rest is the dangerous sea.
- A few islets that were joining the three north-western islands at Azgaar's resolution were removed, so every big island stands alone.

## Where the powers sit (approved by Joshua, 2026-11-08)

- **[[Saraïm]]** holds the **centre**: the biggest island, plus four nearby large islands, so it spans a decent bit more than the others (about 23% of the land). *Which four islands is Claude's draft. Joshua approved the map as drawn.*
- **[[Gibborim]]** holds the **second-biggest island**, in the north-west (about 7%). They have no sea travel, so they live on one island.
- **[[Toldoth]]** holds the **third-biggest island**, to the west between Gibborim and Saraïm (about 7%). It's kept to roughly that island.
- The white islands are **unclaimed** on this map. The Iyim and any neutrals are still TBD.

## Not canon yet

These are **placeholders made by the generator**. They are not decisions:

- The Iyim, Saraïm's internal provinces, [[Zebul]] and [[Pātuna]] aren't placed yet.
- The **climate, biomes and rivers** are Azgaar's simulation, not established lore.
- Azgaar's auto-generated **place names** were hidden from the renders and aren't canon.
