# The Saraïm Priesthood

> The **Sopherim** ("the scribes / keepers") — the priests who *actually* run [[Saraïm]]: quasi-religious historians who venerate an inbred god-emperor while quietly sidelining him, keeping "the Mandate" and crushing the Kilayim. The competent rot at the empire's heart.

## What they are

- A distinct **order within the pureblood [[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]] caste** — not warriors (that's the *sarim* nobles) but **quasi-religious historians and administrators.**
- Their charge: **preserve "the Mandate"** — the Saraïm's founding ideology and bloodline history (descent from [[The Morning Star|Satan]]; the conquest-destiny to rule the stars) — and **enforce the oppression of the [[Kilayim Races|Kilayim]].**
- In practice, they are the **real government** of [[Saraïm]].

## Religion? — propaganda with a priesthood

It's **technically not a religion** — there's no living god to worship (the [[The Morning Star|Morning Star]] is held **dead**). But it **functions as one:** a dogma (pureblood supremacy), a sacred **Mandate** (manifest destiny to conquer the stars, willed by the dead progenitor), a descent-mythology, and a priesthood to keep it. In truth the **Sopherim are fascist propagandists fused with a civil service** — the ministry of the Mandate: they **propagate the creed, write the official history, and run the empire in its name.**

## How the Sopherim are chosen

- **Pure blood only.** The ideology demands it — a Sopher must be **[[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]].**
- **But not the *purest*.** The grim catch: the **purest lines are the most inbred** — degenerate, like the [[Saraïm#The throne (top of the chain)|Melek]] himself. So the Sopherim are drawn from the **functional purebloods**: pure enough to be holy, *sound* enough to think. The best of the blood that still works — the unspoken reason they, not the throne, can actually govern.
- **Chosen young, for intellect.** The sharpest pure children are taken into the order (the martial ones become *sarim* nobles). They're examined and winnowed across **decades of study** — the Mandate's doctrine, the bloodline-histories, law, administration.
- **Set apart, non-hereditary.** A Sopher is **made, not born** to the office and founds **no dynasty** — they serve the **order and the Mandate, not a family.** A self-perpetuating ideological bureaucracy, loyal to the creed and no threat to the throne it quietly manages.

## The doublethink (their hidden inconsistency)

The priests are caught in a contradiction they **deliberately refuse to examine:**

- Their ideology **demands they worship the [[Saraïm#The throne (top of the chain)|emperor]]** — the purest blood, the King-of-Canaan line, the holy apex. And they *do*, sincerely enough.
- But they **know he isn't fit to govern** — an inbred degenerate ([[Nephilim Biology & Caste|the Habsburg curse]] at its worst).
- So they tell themselves they **serve** him by "relieving him of the burden" — taking up more and more administration "on his behalf."
- The truth they hide from: they **encourage the emperor to waste himself** — indulge his vices, chase his whims — precisely so **they** can run the kingdom unobstructed. He *could* command anything and it would happen; they simply make sure he rarely bothers.
- A whole clerical class living in **bad faith**, ruling in the name of a god they keep distracted.

## Why they matter to the story

- They — not the emperor — are the **competent power** that would actually **organize an invasion of Earth.** After the [[Book Plotline|book's regicide]], it's the priests who **hunt the king-killers** and, in time, turn that hunt toward Earth.
- *(Real-world rhyme: a decadent figurehead monarch managed by a self-justifying clerical bureaucracy that "serves" the throne while running everything.)*
