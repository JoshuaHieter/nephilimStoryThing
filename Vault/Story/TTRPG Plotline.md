# TTRPG Plotline

> The **flexible, playable** version. The crew reaches [[The Ocean World|Rephaim]] **quickly** and the campaign opens with the party **fighting the [[Gibborim]]** (ox-centaur Reëmim).

## Continuity

- Shared setup: [[The Pope's Mission]] → [[The Crossing]] → stranding. The game **gets through the setup fast** to reach play.
- Events here are **not bound** to the [[Book Plotline]] — its own continuity, shaped by the players.

## The landing (opening)

- The crew arrives near **[[Gibborim]]** territory and the campaign **opens in combat** against the **Reëmim** (ox-centaur brutes — [[Kilayim Races]]).
- Action-forward start: **guns-vs-giants**, raw survival, outwitting strong-but-dim foes. A strong first-session hook.

## Why this opening works (for a TTRPG)

- **Stakes and combat immediately** — players want to *do* something turn one.
- The Reëmim are ideal early foes: **terrifyingly strong but low-IQ**, so guns + cleverness can win.
- **Sandbox-friendly:** from the badlands the party can push toward the other powers at their own pace.

## Frame / structure

- Likely a **survival sandbox.** (System options were sketched earlier — Year Zero / d100 / grimdark, etc.)
- Beats are **loose and emergent**, not fixed.
