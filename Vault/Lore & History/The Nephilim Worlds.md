# The Nephilim Worlds

> The worlds the Nephilim fled to during the Flood, now grown into distinct civilizations that know of one another.

## Overview

After escaping the Flood ([[Nephilim & the Flood]]), the Nephilim settled a few of [[The Known Worlds]]. Over the generations since, they've become full civilizations.

## What's known

- They **know the real method of crossing** ([[The Crossing]]) — the thing humans completely lack. That's *how* they reached, settled, and still move between worlds, despite the crossing looking like pure luck to everyone on Earth.
- The Nephilim worlds are **in regular contact.** They travel between one another about **as often as people cross between continents on Earth** — an established, ordinary thing, but a real voyage rather than a casual trip.
- They have **adapted and changed to suit their environments.** Different worlds → **different Nephilim cultures** (and likely different forms, customs, beliefs).
- They share at least one common thread: a **prohibition against going to Earth** — though each culture frames it differently. See [[Why Earth Is Forbidden]].
