# History of Rephaim

> From the Flood-flight to the dawn of engines: roughly **3,900 years** of Nephilim on [[The Ocean World|Rephaim]], with a great plague in the middle. Nothing was written down until after that plague.

## Overview

The Nephilim reached Rephaim about **3,900 years** before the book's present (the 1520s on Earth). **Nobody wrote history until after the Wasting**, so everything before it survives only as legend, and every power tells it to suit itself. Recorded history begins in the plague's aftermath, when the races and creeds of Rephaim hardened into what they are today.

## The eras

### 1. The Flight (arrival, ~3,900 years ago)
- Nephilim fleeing **the Flood** blunder through [[The Crossing]] by accident, and the **King of Canaan's** line is among them ([[Nephilim & the Flood]], [[Saraïm#The throne (top of the chain)|the throne]]). They wash up on Rephaim and only later learn the crossing's true method.
- Small **Tahor** households take the best isles and begin breeding with the native stocks. These are the first **[[Kilayim Races|Kilayim]]**.
- *(Legend only: nothing written survives.)*

### 2. The Scattered Lordships
- Centuries of petty **Tahor lords**, each holding an isle or a valley with a few hybrid clans beneath them. No empires.
- Bloodlines still **mix freely**. The races are not yet fixed.
- *(Legend only.)*

### 3. The Wasting (the muddling event)
- A plague that strikes the **[[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Tahorim]] hardest**: generations of inbreeding have left the pure blood with **weak immunity** (real biology).
- **What it leaves behind:**
  - **[[Gibborim]]:** its Tahorim **all die.** This is why the land has none.
  - **[[Toldoth]]:** the survivors conclude **purity is a sickness**, the root of their breeding for balance.
  - **[[Saraïm]]:** the surviving purebloods, the **King of Canaan's line** among them, take their survival as **proof of chosen blood.**
- What little was remembered of earlier days is scrambled. Afterwards the survivors **rebuild the past to suit themselves.**

### 4. The Hardening (written history begins)
- **History begins to be written**, from the survivors' point of view.
- Races and creeds **set**:
  - The first **[[The Saraïm Priesthood|Sopherim]]** write **the Mandate**, filling the lost past with *"we are the heirs of the [[The Morning Star|Morning Star]], destined to rule the stars."*
  - **Toldoth** begins its **lineage ledgers.**
  - **Gibborim** settles into **clans and warbands.**

### 5. Consolidation & Conquest
- **The empires were not always unified.** Saraïm and Toldoth each **consolidate** from rival holdings into single powers, then begin **conquering.**
- **Saraïm** subjugates the Kilayim races into its underclass caste-stack.
- Those who escape or hold out keep to **highly defensible locations**. These become the **[[The Ocean World|Iyim]]**, each one a single-race enclave. **[[Pātuna]]**, still refilled by runaways, is one of them.

### 6. The Dawn of Engines (the present)
- The **early industrial revolution** begins: steam and gearwork (Penuel's coal steamboat is one example; see [[The Ocean World#Technology|Technology]]). Still **no gunpowder.**
- **Things kick off.** *(What exactly: see open questions.)*
