# Nephilim & the Flood

> During the Flood, some Nephilim escaped by crossing the sea-star, and colonized the other worlds.

## Overview

When the **Flood** came, not all the Nephilim were destroyed. Some **escaped** — at first **by accident**, blundering out to sea and across the same way human ships still stumble through today ([[The Crossing]]). But unlike humans, they didn't leave it to luck: over time they **learned how the crossing actually works**, mastered its **real method**, and used it to **deliberately settle all those other worlds** ([[The Known Worlds]]), where they've been ever since.

## What this sets up

- The Nephilim now appearing on Earth's shores (late 1520s) are presumably connected to those escaped colonists, or their descendants. *(How exactly — open.)*
- The other worlds are, in some sense, **theirs** — settled long before any human ship blundered across.

## Connections

- [[The Crossing]] — the method they fled by, still the only way across.
- [[The Nephilim Worlds]] — what their civilizations have become since.
- [[Why Earth Is Forbidden]] — the doctrine born of the Flood: stay away from Earth.
- [[The Outcast Scholar]] — the Nephilim who broke that rule and crossed to Earth in the late 1520s.
- [[Setting Principles]] — the Nephilim are one of the only more-than-natural elements; keep them low-magic / biological-eerie, not fantastical.

## Resolved since

- **A few** worlds, not one — see [[The Nephilim Worlds]].
- They've **adapted to their environments** into distinct cultures, and **know of one another.**
- The Nephilim on Europe's shore in the late 1520s is [[The Outcast Scholar]], who came **on purpose** and died on arrival.
