# The Saraïm Faith

> The religion of [[Saraïm]]: a faith of **waiting**. Their progenitor, the [[The Morning Star|Morning Star]], is not dead but **bound by Yahweh**, and shines over them every night as the **pole star**. His fallen angels wheel around him as the **constellations**.

## Overview

The Saraïm are **more than Tahor supremacists**. They are **genuinely religious**. Blood-pride and the conquest Mandate are real, but they rest on worship of a living, bound lord whose star is the fixed centre of their sky.

## The sky

- **The Morning Star is Earth's sun.** Seen from Rephaim, Earth's sun is a star, and it is their **pole star**, like Polaris but more exact: the **one fixed point** the whole sky turns around.
- **The fallen angels are the constellations**, a court circling their unmoving lord. *(Shemihazah is **not** their leader. The Morning Star is.)*
- The pantheon is **broad strokes**: each fallen angel stands for a wide **aspect** of life.

## Where he is (the main theory)

- **He is not dead, but trapped by Yahweh.** His star still shining is the proof.
- He is bound on **Earth**, which they believe is **still a drowned world, all sea and no land**, as Noah's Flood left it. So he is **out of reach**.
- **The truth:** Earth is **habitable**, and the Morning Star is **seriously reachable** by following his star ([[The Crossing]]). Discovering that would be a **huge deal**: it turns waiting into a **crusade** ([[The Morning Star]], [[Book Plotline]]).
- **Nobody knows Earth survived.** Only [[The Outcast Scholar|Penuel]] suspected it, and he is a **heretic** for it ([[Why Earth Is Forbidden]]).

## Minority views

- **"His light outlives him"** *(heretical)*: he is dead, and the star is only his light still arriving, the way real starlight can outlast its star.
- **[[Toldoth]]:** he is simply **dead**, and not worth worshipping.

## The pantheon

Names are borrowed from 1 Enoch's Watchers, but the aspects are **reshaped into broad strokes**. The pantheon is **not tied to any nationality**: all Saraïm share it.

| Fallen angel | Aspect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Azazel** | War & the Forge | weapons, metal, strength; patron of the new engines |
| **Kokabiel** | the Stars & Voyaging | sailors, navigation, the crossing |
| **Baraqiel** | Storm & Sea | "lightning of God"; weather, the monster-seas |
| **Penemue** | Writing & Memory | taught writing; patron of the [[The Saraïm Priesthood\|Sopherim]] |
| **Sariel** | the Moon, Tides & Death | the dead, endings, the Wasting |
| **Armaros** | Secrets & Cunning | spies, hidden knowledge |
| **Gadreel** | Desire & the Taking of Brides | the descent itself: marriage, fertility, bloodlines |
| **Araqiel** | Land & Dominion | rule, harvest, conquest; fits the Mandate |

## Suggestions (not yet canon): day-to-day practice

- **Tahorim households** keep a house shrine, recite the bloodline, and keep the star-nights.
- **The Sopherim** keep the **star calendar**, read omens in the constellations (sincere but inert, per [[Setting Principles]]), certify births by blood, approve marriages for purity, and officiate deaths.
- **Kilayim laity** venerate the Tahorim as **living relics** of the fallen angels. Their service is their worship. "Unclean" races such as the [[Tunoth]] are **barred from temples**.
- **Festivals:** constellation risings; a night of **vigil for the bound lord**; a remembrance of [[History of Rephaim|the Wasting]] as "the Winnowing."
