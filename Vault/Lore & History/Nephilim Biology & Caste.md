# Nephilim Biology & Caste

> What the Nephilim actually are, across all [[The Known Worlds|three alien worlds]]: a revered caste of inbred half-angel giants, ruling over a sprawling underclass of their own crossbred hybrids.

## Pure Nephilim — the Tahorim (the upper caste)

> Native name: **the Tahorim** — "the Pure" (sing. **Tahor**), from a word for *clean / pure.*

The "true" Nephilim — **about 20%** of the Nephilim population on every world *(a world-wide average, unevenly spread — e.g. [[Gibborim]] has none at all).*

- **What they are:** **half human, half fallen angel.**
- **Stature:** **8–10 feet tall** — strong by sheer size, but usually **lanky.**
- **Mind:** sharp — **~120 IQ average.**
- **The inbreeding problem:** they've bred among themselves so long that **most carry some genetic defect, physical or mental** — *think the average Habsburg.* They *can* turn out normal, but it's common to see **pronounced deformities (Habsburg-style jaws and chins), autism, and borderline insanity is fairly normal.**
- **Low fertility** — they don't breed easily, part of why they stay a 20% minority.
- **Status:** **religiously worshipped, or at least given religious weight**, on every world. In every Nephilim civilization, they are the **upper caste.**

## The hybrids — the Kilayim (the common folk)

> Native name: **the Kilayim** — "the mixed," the old word for *forbidden crossbred kinds.* A label and a slur at once.

- Being **crossbred spawn themselves**, Nephilim can **interbreed far more readily than humans can** — across kinds.
- So they bred with **the native races already present** on these worlds, producing **hybrids** — often **half-animalistic** — who make up the **common folk** (the other ~80%).
- This is why these worlds teem with **"alien types of all sorts":** the underclass is a whole spectrum of Nephilim-native crossbreeds.
- **Traits vary wildly with the native race bred in** — e.g. [[Gibborim]]'s hybrids are monstrously strong but dim; others may be clever, amphibious, winged, whatever the parent stock was. (See the roster in [[Kilayim Races]].)

## Souls & spiritual nature

The Nephilim sit **between humans and demons** — and below both in hope:

- **They have no souls — or if they do, those souls are not redeemable.** What animates them is a **strong but fallen angelic will**, not a soul Christ died to save. There is **no salvation on offer to them: Christ did not die for the Nephilim.**
- In temperament they behave like **poor-spirited people** — like **a sinner convinced he cannot be saved** — but *not* as far gone as a demon.
- They are **like demons, but weaker:** grounded and limited by their **physical form**, and they **do not directly serve Satan.** Lesser, fleshbound, fallen.
- This is what their own doctrine means in calling them **soulless** ([[Why Earth Is Forbidden]]): no soul at all — or none that can be saved.

## Society: cohesion by fear

Their society holds together **mostly by fear — the strong imposing on the weak.** The pure-Nephilim upper caste rules the hybrid majority through **domination, not loyalty or love.** It coheres, but coldly: a hierarchy of dread, fitting a people of strong but poor spirit.

## Why this matters

- A pure-Nephilim body is **unmistakably not human** — an **8-to-10-foot giant**, often deformed. (See [[The Outcast Scholar]].) To a 1500s European that reads straight out of Genesis — *giants*, **Nephilim** — which is exactly why that becomes the **dreaded hypothesis**, not a known fact.
- The **caste-and-worship order** means the marooned crew are moving through a rigid, sacred social hierarchy they don't understand — among a people who treat their giants as holy.
