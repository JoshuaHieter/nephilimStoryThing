# The Morning Star

> Satan — alive, real, and **confined to Earth by God.** He did not know the Nephilim existed until one washed up on his shore. If the Nephilim ever learn he lives, the invasion begins.

## The confinement

- **Satan is real and alive** — the Morning Star, Lucifer, the great adversary of **Yahweh.**
- But **God has confined him to Earth.** He cannot leave it; his reach ends at the world's edge.
- This is *why* he never knew about the Nephilim: when the fallen angels' giant offspring **fled the Flood across [[The Crossing|the star]]** ([[Nephilim & the Flood]]), they passed **beyond his confinement** — out to worlds he can neither see nor touch. For ages **he had no idea his kind's spawn had survived and multiplied out there.**

## The turning point: the scholar

- That changed when **[[The Outcast Scholar]] crossed *to* Earth** — a Nephilim walking, at last, into Satan's domain.
- His arrival is what **reveals the Nephilim to Satan.** The scholar came *seeking* the Morning Star; he was **right** that Satan lived — and his coming is the very thing that tips Satan off to everything beyond his prison.

## The danger — and the crew's real job

- The [[Saraïm]] **rightly** believe he **lives**, **bound by Yahweh on Earth**. To them he is the **pole star** of their sky. But they think Earth is a **drowned world, all sea and no land**, so he is **unreachable**. The [[Toldoth]] think him **dead**, and simply don't care. **No one knows Earth is habitable, or that he can be reached.**
- **If the Nephilim (the Saraïm above all) ever learn that Earth is habitable and the Morning Star is seriously reachable**, he will **influence them to attack Earth.** Now that he knows they exist, that's exactly what he wants: a fallen-angel host, off-world and beyond God's flood, turned against the world that cages him.
- So the mission's **deeper purpose, discovered along the way:** the crew must **make sure they never find out.** Keep the secret, or light the fuse on the invasion ([[The Pope's Mission]]).

## Truth vs. belief

- **Saraïm:** our **progenitor lives, bound** on a drowned Earth, beyond reach, and we his pureblood heirs will rule the stars he won → *half right: he lives and is caged, but Earth **survived**, it is **habitable**, and he is **reachable**.*
- **Toldoth:** a dead god → *wrong; he lives, just caged.*
- Only Earth's people — and now the crew — are placed to know the real shape of it.

## Open questions

- *How* could Satan reach across to influence the Saraïm if he's confined to Earth? (Through the crew? A returning ship? The scholar's body/diary? Whispers along the fallen-angel blood?)
- Does he already know **where** the Nephilim are, or only *that* they are?
- Why did God confine him to **Earth** specifically — and does the cage weaken if someone carries his influence outward?
- Did the scholar **reach him at all** before dying, or only die on the threshold?
