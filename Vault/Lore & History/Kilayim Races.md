# Kilayim Races

> The hybrid "common folk" of the Nephilim worlds — the [[Nephilim Biology & Caste|Kilayim]] — bred from the native races the Nephilim crossed with. These are Rephaim's "alien types of all sorts."

> [!note] Design rule
> Every Kilayim's animal stock is a **real Earth animal *family*** — but a **genus and species found nowhere on Earth.** They read as *plausible* (a bovid, a corvid, a canid) yet alien. See [[Setting Principles]].

## Reëmim — ox-centaurs ([[Gibborim]])

- **Stock:** family **Bovidae** (cattle / wild oxen) — an alien wild-ox genus, *not* Earth's aurochs.
- **Form:** centaur-like — a towering humanoid torso on the body of a massive wild bull.
- **Traits:** **monstrously strong**, slow-witted — the brute power of [[Gibborim]].
- **Culture (Celtic / Māori warrior-tribe):** clan-bound; they **raid each other's herds, take heads, and boast in war-chant.** Honor and revenge (*utu*) run their world.
- **Name:** Heb. *re'em*, the wild ox.

## Ayalim — stag-folk ([[Toldoth]])

- **Stock:** family **Cervidae** — but the base creature is the **ayal**, an **alien deer found nowhere on Earth.** In form it's akin to a **great white stag** — large, pale, majestic (echoing the Celtic white hart) — but its **antlers are not bone: they are living, clear, faintly-refracting crystal**, like living glass somewhere between coral and quartz. Unmistakably not of Earth.
- **The gradient — blood you read at a glance:** pure **Tahor** = towering, pale, **antlerless.** The more Kilayim, the **larger and more branching the antlers**, plus pelt, hooved legs, swept ears, deer-eyes. **Rank crowns your head** — and because those antlers are **crystalline**, a great branching rack literally **glitters**: the ultimate, unmissable status display. Unlike most Kilayim (who stop near half-animal), the Ayalim grade all the way to a near-full **"great stag."**
- **The trade-off:** more animal → keener senses, speed, grace, hardiness; **less of the prized intellect.** The pure rule by mind — *usually* — but a sharp deep-hybrid can still rise ([[Toldoth]]'s "not always").
- **The wild ayal:** the same alien deer **roams the Toldoth isles wild, in herds.** Not sacred — but **killing or eating one is deep taboo**, the way eating a pet dog would be in America: too close to kin, too much a companion. *(A brutal snag for a starving, marooned crew — see [[Toldoth]].)*
- **Name:** Heb. *ayal*, the hart.

## The Saraïm underclass

The empire of [[Saraïm]] runs on a **caste-stack of many** Kilayim races, each slotted into the imperial machine. A starter roster:

### Chazirim — boar-folk
- **Stock:** family **Suidae** (pigs / boars), alien genus. **Name:** Heb. *chazir*, "swine."
- **Role:** brute labor — miners, haulers, shock-troops. Tusked, thick, tireless.

### Kelevim — hound-folk
- **Stock:** family **Canidae** (dogs / wolves), alien genus. **Name:** Heb. *kelev*, "dog."
- **Role:** trackers, soldiers, loyal servants — the empire's leashed muscle and noses.

### Tunoth — eel-folk
- **Stock:** family **Anguillidae** (eels), alien genus. **Name:** Māori *tuna*, "eel."
- **Form:** short, skinny, black-skinned, big eel eyes and toothy maw — **goblinish**; low IQ; slime-slick and amphibious.
- **Role:** dock, dive, and sea labor — the **"unclean" bottom caste** of [[Saraïm]]; slippery survivors.
- **→ Full write-up: [[Tunoth]].**

### Orevim — raven-folk
- **Stock:** family **Corvidae** (crows / ravens / jays), alien genus. **Name:** Heb. *orev*, "raven."
- **Role:** clever and servile — scribes, messengers, spies. The sharpest of the underclass, and the most quietly dangerous.

## The Iyim (free enclaves)

The island city-states of the [[The Ocean World|Iyim]] are a scatter of **weaker, independent city-states — each populated *wholly* by a single [[Saraïm]]-underclass race.** The same race that is crushed chattel under Saraïm lives **free, but weak,** on its own isle: an isle of Chazirim, an isle of Tunoth, an isle of Orevim, and so on. Built-in oppressed-vs-free-diaspora tension. *(Individual isles TBD.)*
