# Setting Principles

> The rules the rest of the setting bends to. When something contradicts these, these win.

## The thesis

**This story is an excuse to have Christians fight aliens.** That is the heart of it. Everything else serves that.

## The series theme: Rome vs. Judea

*(Joshua's framing, close to his words.)*

- There has been **enmity between Christ and the people who killed and abandoned him.** **Rome** was chosen as the empire to destroy the **Second Temple**, and there has been a struggle between the Jews and **Rome's successors** ever since.
- In our world that has been a **small, disparate group** against a **more powerful but less cunning power**.
- **The series asks:** what if, instead of **bigotry and hatred toward kin**, it were a **real, more evenly matched struggle between two totally irreconcilable groups**?
- So the series is an **extension of Rome vs. Judea.** The Nephilim side is coded through **Hebrew and its Semitic sister-languages** (no Indo-European naming), set against Rome and its successors.

## Magic level: very, very low

- This is **not** a fantasy world. No spellcasting, no wizards, no working sorcery.
- The most "otherworldly" thing you'll meet on the far side is **weird alien *biological* creatures** — strange life, not magic. Test: **a middle-schooler in biology class could believe in them.** "Monsters," sure — but no more magical than Earth's own monsters.
- **Taxonomy rule:** every alien creature belongs to a **real Earth animal *family*** — but a **genus and species found nowhere on Earth** (a bovid, a corvid, a canid you've never seen). Plausible, but not of Earth. See [[Kilayim Races]].
- The only genuinely more-than-natural elements in the whole setting are:
  - the **crossing-star** phenomenon ([[The Crossing]]), and
  - the **Nephilim** themselves ([[Nephilim & the Flood]]).
  - Even these read as eerie / biological rather than wizardly.

## The "voodoo" is not real magic

- The [[The Voodoo Pirates|voodoo pirates]] **sincerely believe** in what they do.
- But **nothing supernatural actually happens** when they practice it. The rituals are mechanically inert.
- What's real about it is **spiritual**, not magical: they're being **tricked into deeper sin**, not channeling any power.
