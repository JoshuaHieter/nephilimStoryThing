# The Known Worlds

> A few worlds are reachable by [[The Crossing]]. None is truly hospitable — the star drops you at the one spot on each that's close to a normal habitat.

## The shape of a world

- There are **three** known alien worlds.
- **Each world leans heavily toward a single biome**, and is **largely harsh** — most of it not fit for normal life.
- The **most habitable spot on each world is the continent the Nephilim hold** — and that is exactly **where the crossing-star deposits you** ([[The Crossing]]). You arrive in Nephilim territory.
- Within that continent: **lands, nations, and factions** (specifics vary by world).

So the playable geography:

```
Earth + three alien worlds (one crossing-star each)
  each alien world:
  └── one dominant biome, mostly harsh
      └── the Nephilim continent — the most habitable spot, where you arrive
          └── nations & factions
```

## The three worlds

1. **[[The Ocean World]]** *(name TBD)* — biome: **ocean**; an archipelago of large islands. The **most civilized, advanced, and least hostile** of the three. **Where the crew goes.**
2. **Second world** — *biome TBD.* Untouched in canon so far.
3. **Third world** — *biome TBD.* Untouched in canon so far.

From Earth, that's **three world-stars** in the sky ([[The Crossing]]) — one per world.

## Who's out there

- The Nephilim hold worlds of their own — see [[The Nephilim Worlds]].
- The non-habitable stretches are home to **weird alien biological life** (per [[Setting Principles]] — biological, not magical).
