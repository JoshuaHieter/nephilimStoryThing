# Home

The home page for this setting. Everything links out from here.

> [!note] Title
> **Series: *Of Giants and Grasshoppers*** — from Numbers 13:33 ("we were like grasshoppers… and so we were in their sight"): humanity against the Nephilim. Individual books are titled by **the year they take place** (Book 1: ***1528***).

> [!important] North star
> **This story is an excuse to have Christians fight aliens.** Very low magic. See [[Lore & History/Setting Principles|Setting Principles]].
